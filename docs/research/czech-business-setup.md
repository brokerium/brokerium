# Czech business setup, IP ownership and trademark fees

Research date: 2026-10-18

Not legal or tax advice. The owner lives in Czechia; these notes collect the rules that matter for
running Brokerium as a business there. Check them with a Czech lawyer or accountant before
signing anything.

## Who owns the code

- Economic rights of an author cannot be transferred or waived; they can only be licensed
  (§ 26(1) of the Copyright Act 121/2000 Sb.). Moving Brokerium into a company later therefore
  means an exclusive licence from the author (or an assignment of the exercise of rights), drafted
  by a lawyer, not a sale of the copyright.
- Works created to fulfil job duties are employee works: the employer exercises the economic
  rights (§ 58(1)). Computer programs created on commission are treated the same way, with the
  client as the employer (§ 58(7)). Code written in personal time, on personal equipment and
  outside job duties stays with the author.
- Code generated by AI without creative human input is not a copyrighted work; documenting the
  human contribution (decisions, design, review) matters for the licence and for a buyer's due
  diligence. See also the AI row in [sale-readiness.md](../legal/sale-readiness.md).

Sources: [Copyright Act, full text](https://www.sagit.cz/_texty/sb00121.htm),
[§ 58 commentary](https://www.kurzy.cz/zakony/121-2000-autorsky-zakon/paragraf-58/),
[Sedláková Legal: who owns software source code](https://www.sedlakovalegal.cz/komu-patri-zdrojovy-kod-software).

## Side business while employed

§ 304(1) of the Labour Code: an employee may carry out gainful activity identical to the
employer's line of business only with the employer's prior written consent. "Identical" means the
same activity, not merely competing or similar; holding shares in or managing a company that does
it counts, even without profit (Supreme Court 21 Cdo 1132/2014). The owner's employer has no
connection to Kafka tooling (owner's statement, 2026-10-18), so no consent is needed.

Sources: [§ 304 text](https://www.pracepropravniky.cz/zakony/zakonik-prace-uplne-zneni/paragraf-304/),
[epravo.cz: side activity of an employee](https://www.epravo.cz/top/clanky/vedlejsi-cinnost-zamestnance-114841.html).

## Trademark offices and fees (2026)

| Route | Territory | Fee | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ÚPV (Czech national mark) | Czechia | 5 000 CZK for up to 3 classes, 500 CZK per further class | [ÚPV fees](https://upv.gov.cz/prumyslova-prava/poplatky) |
| EUIPO (EU trade mark) | All 27 EU states | 850 EUR for 1 class online, 50 EUR for the 2nd, 150 EUR for each further class | Valid 10 years, renewable. [EUIPO fees](https://www.euipo.europa.eu/en/trade-marks/before-applying/fees-payments) |
| WIPO Madrid (international registration) | Chosen member countries | Basic fee plus a fee per designated country | Needs a base application (for example the EU mark); priority of the first filing can be claimed within 6 months |

- No mark protects a name everywhere: protection is per territory and per class of goods and
  services. A business in another class (for example real estate, class 36) is not affected.
- Classes for Brokerium: 9 (downloadable software) and 42 (software services, SaaS).
- [SME Fund 2026](https://www.euipo.europa.eu/en/sme-corner/sme-fund/2026): 75 % reimbursement of
  EU and national trademark fees and 50 % of fees outside the EU, at most 700 EUR per SME. The
  trade mark voucher for 2026 is exhausted; the fund has run every year since 2021. Sole traders
  count as SMEs; the voucher must be granted before filing.
