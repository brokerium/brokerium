# Brokerium Corporate Contributor License Agreement

Version 1.0, 2026-10-15

This Corporate Contributor License Agreement ("Agreement") lets a company or other
legal entity ("Corporation") authorize its employees and contractors to contribute
to Brokerium. It uses the same terms as the [Individual CLA](CLA.md), granted by the
Corporation instead of an individual.

This text has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer; it may be replaced by a
lawyer-reviewed version, which will apply only to Contributions submitted after it is
published.

## 1. Definitions

The definitions of "Maintainer", "Project", "Contribution" and "Submit" from the
[Individual CLA](CLA.md) apply. In addition:

- **"Corporation"** means the legal entity signing this Agreement and all entities
  that control, are controlled by, or are under common control with it.
- **"Designated Employees"** means the employees and contractors listed by the
  Corporation as authorized to Submit Contributions on its behalf. The Corporation
  keeps this list current by notifying the Maintainer.

## 2. Grants

For every Contribution Submitted by a Designated Employee, the Corporation grants the
Maintainer, and recipients of software distributed by the Maintainer, the copyright
license in section 2 and the patent license in section 3 of the
[Individual CLA](CLA.md), including the right to distribute Contributions under any
license terms the Maintainer chooses and to change those terms at any time.

## 3. Representations

The Corporation represents that:

1. It is legally entitled to grant these licenses and the person signing is authorized
   to sign on its behalf.
2. Each Contribution is an original creation of a Designated Employee, or any
   third-party material is identified together with its source and license.
3. Designated Employees who use AI tools review the generated material and do not
   knowingly submit third-party material under incompatible terms.

The Corporation agrees to notify the Maintainer when a representation becomes
inaccurate or a Designated Employee leaves the list.

## 4. Other terms

Sections 5 (moral rights), 6 (what does not change) and 7 (assignment) of the
[Individual CLA](CLA.md) apply to the Corporation and its Designated Employees.

## 5. How to sign

Write to [licensing@brokerium.kaufen](mailto:licensing@brokerium.kaufen) with the subject
"Corporate CLA: <Corporation name>". The maintainer will send the signing form. Designated
Employees still confirm the Individual CLA through the bot so that every pull request is
traceable.
