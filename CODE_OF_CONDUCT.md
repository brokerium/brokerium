# Code of Conduct

Brokerium is used by people who keep critical systems running. We want every
conversation about it to be useful, respectful and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Stay on topic and be specific: logs, versions and reproduction steps help more than
  frustration.
- Accept that the maintainer makes the final decision on features and changes.
- Respect privacy: do not post credentials, customer data or other people's personal
  information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Threats, intimidation or encouraging others to harm anyone.
- Spam, advertising and repeated off-topic posts.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock discussions, and block people who
violate this code, temporarily or permanently. To report a problem privately, use
[GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
or contact the maintainer through a discussion marked "Moderation request" without
sharing details publicly.

This code applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and any
other channel run by the project.
